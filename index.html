How the pieces connect

Box.asm is assembled by the two-pass assembler into Box.hack (16-bit words). The emulator loads that ROM and runs it cycle by cycle. The CPU reads the keyboard at RAM[24576] and writes pixels to screen memory at RAM[16384..24575].
